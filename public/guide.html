🎮Getting started

Pokémon Idle is an idle/incremental game: battles run automatically and your team keeps fighting while you watch. Pick a region and one of its three starters; every region has its own level 5–75 curve, so a fresh region is a fresh climb.

Your progress saves automatically to this browser — there's nothing to install and no account to make.

🔁The core loop

  • Battle the wild Pokémon on your current route; every win earns EXP and money.
  • Wins on a route unlock the next one — some stairs also want badges, a big enough Pokédex, a team level, or a specific catch.
  • Catch Pokémon to grow your Pokédex (which powers the Shiny Charm).
  • Level your team, beat the region's gyms and league, then open another region.

🗺️Regions & Unlock Tokens

There are nine regions, each reproducing its real map of towns and routes. You start in the region you picked; the rest are opened with Region Unlock Tokens.

  • Beating any region's Champion earns a token.
  • Open the Town Map and click a locked region tab (it glows while you hold a token) to unlock it.

🔄Prestige

Once you've finished a region (Champion beaten), the Gyms tab offers Prestige: replay the region from the start — routes and badges reset — without losing your Pokémon, items, money or Pokédex. Each prestige raises the region's level and awards Victory Tokens. Its Elite Four reset too, so you must beat them again before the next prestige.

🌿Wild encounters & catching

  • Every route uses its real wild table; the Wild Pokémon grid shows each species, its type, level range and rarity.
  • The ◆ badge marks species that spawn only on that route.
  • Catch settings decide what gets auto-caught: always, only shinies, above a level, or only species new to the Pokédex — set globally or per route.
  • Balls: Poké Ball, Great Ball, Ultra Ball and the never-failing Master Ball.

⚔️Battles

  • The familiar type chart, STAB, critical hits and stat stages all apply.
  • Status conditions: poison and burn deal damage each turn (burn halves physical attack), freeze and sleep stop a Pokémon acting, and flinching skips a turn. Drain moves heal the user.
  • Moves are learned by levelling; open a Pokémon's stats and use the move manager to teach it anything from its family's learnset.
  • Use the speed controls to fast-forward, and the catch selector to batch-apply Repel/Honey.

🏅Gyms, Elite Four & Champion

Each region has eight gyms, an Elite Four and a Champion. Badges gate the roads onward, and the league asks for all eight before it will let you challenge it. Beating a Champion is what opens the next region.

Leaders field much stronger rematch teams once you have every badge in their region, and the league does the same once the Champion is beaten.

🛒Items & shops

  • Poké Marts (in towns) sell balls and repels; their stock opens up as you progress, so Ultra Balls aren't available in the first town.
  • Victory Tokens — earned from gyms, Champions, Prestige and the Battle Tower — are spent in the Reward Shop.
  • The Reward Shop stocks evolution items: elemental stones, King's Rock, Metal Coat, Protector, Upgrade, the Link Cable (trade evolutions) and more.
  • Repel halves a chosen species' encounter rate; Honey doubles it. Exp. Share levels the whole party.

✨Evolution

Most Pokémon evolve by level, but many need an item: stone evolutions (Pikachu, Eevee, Vulpix, Growlithe, Gloom…), and Pokémon that normally evolve by trading (Kadabra, Machoke, Graveler, Haunter and friends) use a Link Cable.

Open a Pokémon's stats panel to see every evolution it can make and what it needs.

🌊Raid Islands

Every region has a floating Raid Island holding the Pokémon you can't find on any of its routes — including its legendaries. One raid at a time, with a short cooldown between attempts.

🥚Daycare & breeding

  • Deposit two Pokémon from their stats panel into the Daycare; they produce an Egg after 50 battles.
  • Breeding respects gender and egg groups: the parents must be opposite genders and share an egg group. Ditto can breed with almost anything, genderless Pokémon need a Ditto, and Undiscovered species (legendaries, babies) won't breed at all.
  • Eggs hatch into the mother's base form at level 1, with 1-in-4,096 shiny odds — improved to 1-in-512 if a parent is shiny.

🔗Wonder Trade

Spend a Link Cable on any party or box Pokémon to trade it for a random Pokémon at the same level — with a boosted chance of a shiny. Avoid it for your last party member (the trade is refused).

Rumour has it the trade gives back something special if you trade away a shiny.

🏰Battle Tower

Once a region's Champion is beaten, its Gyms tab opens the Battle Tower: endless, increasingly strong trainers for Victory Tokens, with your team healed between floors. The first floors cap out at level 100 around floor 25, after which the trainers' stats keep climbing instead, so it never truly plateaus. A loss ends the run and records your best floor.

🌟Shiny hunting

  • Base shiny odds are 1 in 8,192; the Shiny Charm roughly doubles them.
  • The shiny chain improves the odds of a species you encounter repeatedly (up to 4×), and resets when a different species appears — the chained species shows a ✦ badge.
  • Honey doubles a species' encounter rate and Repel halves one, so you can steer a route toward the Pokémon you're hunting.

🐝Swarms & roamers

  • Swarms: occasionally one of a route's rarer species becomes very common there for 50 battles. The Wild Pokémon header shows an active swarm.
  • Roaming legendaries: a region's uncaught legends can appear on any of its routes and may flee mid-battle — catch them fast, and once caught they leave the roaming pool.

📊Records & achievements

The Records panel (in the Box) summarises your journey: battles won, Pokédex and shiny progress, badges, Victory Tokens, per-region completion and prestige, and your best Tower floor. It also tracks 21 achievements.

📖Pokédex & Shiny Charm

The Pokédex has tabs per generation. Completing a generation's national dex unlocks the Shiny Charm for that generation's species — so the more you catch, the better everyone's shiny odds get.

🔎Encounter index

Looking for a specific Pokémon? The Wild Encounter Index is a searchable, offline page listing every species, level range and rate for every location across all nine regions.

💾Saves

Your game saves automatically to this browser. Settings (the gear / ? menu) has a reset option if you ever want to start over. Back up or sync options are planned.

There are a few hidden things in the world that this guide won't spoil. Explore everywhere, and keep an eye on the battle log…
