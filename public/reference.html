How battles work

Battles run themselves: the two Pokémon trade moves until one faints. This page is the engine's honest inventory — what each move and ability actually does in this game, and which ones are still cosmetic.

Use the Moves, Abilities and Held Items tabs above, or search them directly. Rows tagged inert have no battle effect yet; attack moves simply deal damage.

Damage & turn order

  • The real type chart, same-type attack bonus (STAB), critical hits and an 85–100% damage roll all apply.
  • Stat stages run from −6 to +6; accuracy and evasion are tracked too.
  • Turn order is by Speed, then by move priority. Tailwind, Trick Room, paralysis and the weather/terrain speed abilities all adjust it.
  • Physical moves make contact unless they are projectiles or ground moves, which matters for contact abilities.

Status conditions

  • Poison deals 1/8 max HP each turn; badly poisoned grows from 1/16 upward each turn.
  • Burn deals 1/16 each turn and halves physical Attack.
  • Sleep and freeze stop the Pokémon acting; sleep counts down, freeze has a 20% thaw chance each turn.
  • Paralysis halves Speed and has a 25% chance to skip a turn.
  • Type and ability immunities apply (Fire can't burn, Electric can't be paralysed, and so on).

Volatile & field effects

  • Confusion (2–5 turns, 33% self-hit), infatuation (Attract), flinching and Curse.
  • Leech Seed drains 1/8 max HP into the other side; Ingrain and Aqua Ring heal the holder each turn.
  • Substitute absorbs damage, Protect blocks the turn, Taunt blocks status moves, and Encore/Disable control the target's moves.
  • Screens (Reflect, Light Screen, Aurora Veil), safeguard, mist and Tailwind/Trick Room.
  • Entry hazards: Stealth Rock, Spikes, Toxic Spikes and Sticky Web hit Pokémon as they switch in.

Weather & terrain

  • Rain boosts Water and weakens Fire; sun does the reverse. Both last five turns.
  • Sandstorm chips non-Rock/Ground/Steel Pokémon; snow chips non-Ice Pokémon. The primal weathers and strong winds add their own rules.
  • Terrain (electric, grassy, misty, psychic) boosts its type for grounded Pokémon, and Grassy Terrain heals them each turn.

Abilities, held items & moves

Every Pokémon rolls one of its real abilities on creation, and the stats panel shows it. 250 of the 304 abilities in the data have a real effect — the rest are rolled and displayed but do nothing. The same goes for moves: any status move whose effect the engine doesn't recognise is inert.

A few abilities are simplified. For example the type-absorbing abilities heal 1/4 max HP in this engine rather than raising a stat, and Hustle gives its Attack boost without the accuracy penalty. The per-ability text below describes what this engine does, not the official games.
