🎮Getting started

Pokémon Idle is an idle/incremental game: battles run automatically and your team keeps fighting while you watch. Pick a region and one of its three starters; every region has its own level 5–75 curve, so a fresh region is a fresh climb.

Your progress saves automatically to this browser — there's nothing to install and no account to make.

🔁The core loop

  • Battle the wild Pokémon on your current route; every win earns EXP and money.
  • Wins on a route unlock the next one — some stairs also want badges, a big enough Pokédex, a team level, or a specific catch.
  • Catch Pokémon to grow your Pokédex (which powers the Shiny Charm).
  • Level your team, beat the region's gyms and league, then open another region.

🗺️Regions & Unlock Tokens

There are nine regions, each reproducing its real map of towns and routes. You start in the region you picked; the rest are opened with Region Unlock Tokens.

  • Beating any region's Champion earns a token.
  • Open the Town Map and click a locked region tab (it glows while you hold a token) to unlock it.

🔄Prestige

Once you've finished a region (Champion beaten), the Gyms tab offers Prestige: replay the region from the start — routes and badges reset — without losing your Pokémon, items, money or Pokédex. Each prestige raises the region's level and awards Victory Tokens. Its Elite Four reset too, so you must beat them again before the next prestige.

🌿Wild encounters & catching

  • Every route uses its real wild table; the Wild Pokémon grid shows each species, its type, level range and rarity.
  • The ◆ badge marks species that spawn only on that route.
  • Catch settings decide what gets auto-caught: always, only shinies, above a level, or only species new to the Pokédex — set globally or per route.
  • Balls: Poké Ball, Great Ball, Ultra Ball, Premier Ball, the situational Quick / Timer / Repeat / Net / Dive / Dusk / Nest Balls, and the never-failing Master Ball. Each situational ball only gets its catch bonus in the right situation — see What works & what's WIP.

⚔️Battles

  • The familiar type chart, STAB, critical hits, damage variance and stat stages all apply — plus accuracy and evasion stages.
  • Status conditions: poison and toxic deal damage each turn, burn also halves physical attack, freeze and sleep stop a Pokémon acting, paralysis halves Speed and can skip a turn, confusion can make a Pokémon hurt itself, and flinching skips a turn. Leech Seed drains into the planter.
  • Weather (rain, sun, sandstorm, snow, hail) and terrain (electric, grassy, misty, psychic) scale damage, power abilities and add healing, chip damage or status/priority protection each turn.
  • Abilities and held items are live, but only some are implemented — see What works & what's WIP.
  • Moves are learned by levelling; open a Pokémon's stats and use the Manage Moves panel to re-learn its family's moves for free, or pay the Move Tutor ($4,000 per move) to teach a TM it can learn.
  • Use the speed controls to fast-forward, and the catch selector to batch-apply Repel/Honey.

🏅Gyms, Elite Four & Champion

Each region has eight gyms, an Elite Four and a Champion. Badges gate the roads onward, and the league asks for all eight before it will let you challenge it. Beating a Champion is what opens the next region.

Leaders field much stronger rematch teams once you have every badge in their region, and the league does the same once the Champion is beaten.

🛒Items & shops

  • Poké Marts (in towns) sell balls, potions, repels and held items; their stock opens up as you progress, so Ultra Balls aren't available in the first town.
  • Victory Tokens — earned from gyms, Champions, Prestige and the Battle Tower — are spent in the Reward Shop.
  • The Reward Shop stocks evolution items: elemental stones, King's Rock, Metal Coat, Protector, Upgrade, the Link Cable (trade evolutions) and more.
  • Held items (Leftovers, Choice items, Berries, type boosters…) are equipped from the Bag's Held Items tab. Their effects are listed in What works & what's WIP.
  • Repel halves a chosen species' encounter rate; Honey doubles it. Exp. Share levels the whole party.

✨Evolution

Most Pokémon evolve by level, but many need an item: stone evolutions (Pikachu, Eevee, Vulpix, Growlithe, Gloom…), and Pokémon that normally evolve by trading (Kadabra, Machoke, Graveler, Haunter and friends) use a Link Cable.

Open a Pokémon's stats panel to see every evolution it can make and what it needs. Some evolutions need high friendship instead of a level (Golbat → Crobat, Pichu → Pikachu, Eevee's Espeon / Umbreon / Sylveon, and several others) — friendship rises as the Pokémon levels up and wins battles.

🌊Raid Islands

Every region has a floating Raid Island holding the Pokémon you can't find on any of its routes — including its legendaries. One raid at a time, with a short cooldown between attempts.

🥚Daycare & breeding

  • Deposit two Pokémon from their stats panel into the Daycare; they produce an Egg after 50 battles.
  • Breeding respects gender and egg groups: the parents must be opposite genders and share an egg group. Ditto can breed with almost anything, genderless Pokémon need a Ditto, and Undiscovered species (legendaries, babies) won't breed at all.
  • Eggs hatch into the mother's base form at level 1, with 1-in-4,096 shiny odds — improved to 1-in-512 if a parent is shiny. The Egg goes to your party (or box when full) and hatches after 30–120 wild wins depending on the species.
  • Eggs inherit from their parents: 3 of the parents' best IVs, a 50% chance of a parent's nature, and any moves the hatchling can learn that the parents knew.

🔗Wonder Trade

Spend a Link Cable on any party or box Pokémon to trade it for a random Pokémon at the same level — with a boosted chance of a shiny. Avoid it for your last party member (the trade is refused).

Rumour has it the trade gives back something special if you trade away a shiny.

🏰Battle Tower

The Battle Tower lives in Hoenn. Once Hoenn's Champion is beaten, its Gyms tab opens the tower: endless, increasingly strong trainers for Victory Tokens, with your team healed between floors. Stats start scaling at floor 15, the pay is one token per ten floors, and a loss ends the run and records your best floor.

Tower battles are a deterministic puzzle: each floor's six opponents are fixed and there are no critical hits, misses or damage variance, so the result comes down to your team's stats, types, moves, abilities and held items. The Gyms tab previews the next floor so you can build a counter before committing. Other regions are planned to get their own minigames instead.

🌟Shiny hunting

  • Base shiny odds are 1 in 8,192; the Shiny Charm roughly doubles them.
  • The shiny chain improves the odds of a species you encounter repeatedly (up to 4×), and resets when a different species appears — the chained species shows a ✦ badge.
  • Honey doubles a species' encounter rate and Repel halves one. Once the chain reaches 4 they lock the hunt in: Honey heavily favours the hunted species and Repel pushes every other species away, so a chain can't be broken by a random encounter.

🐝Swarms & roamers

  • Swarms: occasionally one of a route's rarer species becomes very common there for 50 battles. The Wild Pokémon header shows an active swarm.
  • Roaming legendaries: a region's uncaught legends can appear on any of its routes and may flee mid-battle — catch them fast, and once caught they leave the roaming pool.

📊Records & achievements

The Records panel (in the Box) summarises your journey: battles won, Pokédex and shiny progress, badges, Victory Tokens, per-region completion and prestige, and your best Tower floor. It also tracks 21 achievements.

📖Pokédex & Shiny Charm

The Pokédex has tabs per generation. Completing a generation's national dex unlocks the Shiny Charm for that generation's species — so the more you catch, the better everyone's shiny odds get.

🔎Encounter index

Looking for a specific Pokémon? The Wild Encounter Index is a searchable, offline page listing every species, level range and rate for every location across all nine regions.

💾Saves

Your game saves automatically to this browser. Settings (the gear / ? menu) can export your save to a file and import one back, or reset to start over. Cloud sync is planned.

🧪What works & what's WIP

The engine is deep but not complete, and a few battle features are easy to misread. This page lists what is fully implemented, what is only partly there, and the quirks worth knowing about — especially moves and abilities.

Moves — what actually does something

A move only works if its effect is one the engine understands. Any move whose effect falls outside the list below behaves like a plain attack (if it has power) or does nothing at all (if it is a status move). There are roughly 900 moves in the data, so many status moves are still cosmetic.

  • Damage moves work, including STAB, the type chart, critical hits and the 85–100% damage roll.
  • Stat changes (Swords Dance, Growl, Calm Mind, Agility…), including the accuracy and evasion stages used by Sand Attack / Double Team.
  • Status: poison, badly poisoned (toxic), burn, freeze, sleep, paralysis, confusion, flinching, infatuation (Attract) and Curse.
  • Healing (Recover, Roost, Synthesis, Rest…) and drain moves (Giga Drain, Leech Life).
  • Multi-hit moves (Bullet Seed, Icicle Spear…) and high critical-hit moves (Slash, Stone Edge…).
  • Protect / Detect (and King's Shield-style blocking), Taunt, Substitute, Leech Seed and the stat reset of Haze / Clear Smog.
  • Screens: Reflect, Light Screen, Aurora Veil.
  • Entry hazards: Stealth Rock, Spikes, Toxic Spikes, Sticky Web — they hit Pokémon as they switch in.
  • Weather: Rain Dance, Sunny Day, Sandstorm, Snowscape and Hail.
  • Terrain: Electric, Grassy, Misty and Psychic Terrain.
  • Recoil, crash-on-miss, recharge (Hyper Beam), self-destruct and multi-turn locks (Outrage-style) are handled.
  • Move Tutor: the Manage Moves panel lists the TMs a Pokemon can learn (its real machine move set) and teaches them for $4,000 each.
Not yet implemented: Taunt's full interaction rules and the large tail of one-off utility moves. If it is not in the list above, assume it does little.

Abilities — the implemented set

Every Pokémon rolls one of its real abilities (there is a small chance of the hidden one) and the stats panel shows it. Only about 40 of the ~300 abilities have an effect so far; the rest are inert. The working ones are:

  • Entry: Intimidate, Drizzle, Drought, Sand Stream, Snow Warning, Electric Surge, Grassy Surge, Misty Surge, Psychic Surge.
  • Type immunity / absorb: Levitate, Water Absorb, Dry Skin, Volt Absorb, Lightning Rod, Motor Drive, Flash Fire, Well-Baked Body, Sap Sipper, Storm Drain.
  • Damage: Thick Fat, Multiscale, Shadow Shield, Filter, Solid Rock, Prism Armor, Wonder Guard, Magic Guard, Sturdy, Overgrow, Blaze, Torrent, Swarm.
  • Stats: Huge Power, Pure Power, Guts, Speed Boost, Clear Body, White Smoke, Hyper Cutter, Big Pecks, Keen Eye.
  • Status & weather: Immunity, Limber, Water Veil, Insomnia, Vital Spirit, Sweet Veil, Magma Armor, Shed Skin, Swift Swim, Chlorophyll, Sand Rush, Slush Rush, Rain Dish, Dry Skin, Ice Body.
  • Contact (when hit): Static, Flame Body, Poison Point, Effect Spore, Cute Charm, Rough Skin, Iron Barbs.
  • Critical hits: Super Luck, Sniper, Battle Armor, Shell Armor.
Most switch-out abilities (Regenerator, Natural Cure) and the large tail of situational abilities are not wired up yet.

Held items

Held items are bought at marts and equipped from the Bag's Held Items tab; wild and trainer Pokémon hold them too. These work:

  • Leftovers (heal 1/16 a turn) and Life Orb (+30% damage, 10% recoil).
  • Choice Band / Specs / Scarf (+50% stat) and Assault Vest (+50% Sp. Def). Note: the Choice items do not lock you into one move yet.
  • Eviolite (+50% Def/Sp. Def unless fully evolved), Focus Sash (survive one KO from full HP).
  • Sitrus Berry and Lum Berry.
  • All 18 type boosters (Charcoal, Mystic Water, Magnet, the plates…), giving +20% to their type.

Other things that trip people up

  • Battle Tower is Hoenn-only and is a deterministic puzzle: each floor's opponent is fixed and there are no crits, misses or damage variance. The Gyms tab previews the next floor so you can build a counter. Other regions will get their own minigames later.
  • Situational balls only get their bonus in the right situation — Quick (one-turn battle), Timer (long battle), Repeat (already in the Pokédex), Net (Water/Bug), Dive (water routes), Dusk (caves/night) and Nest (low level). Auto-catch picks the best one.
  • Honey + Repel become a real hunt once the shiny chain reaches 4: Honey heavily favours the hunted species and Repel pushes every other species away.
  • Friendship (a ❤ badge in the stats panel) rises with levels and wild wins, and gates friendship evolutions such as Golbat → Crobat and Eevee's Espeon / Umbreon / Sylveon.
  • Breeding inherits 3 of the parents' best IVs, a 50% chance of a parent's nature, and moves the hatchling can learn.
  • Challenge modes (Settings): Randomizer (random wild species), Level Scaling (wild levels match your active Pokemon) and Nuzlocke (a fainted party Pokemon is released for good \u2014 your last one is spared).
